How far is Udaipur Airport from the main city?

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDA) is about 22km from the Udaipur City Palace and main tourist areas. It usually takes 45-60 minutes depending on traffic.

Yes, we operate 24/7. Whether your flight lands at 2:00 AM or 11:00 PM, your driver will be waiting for you.

No. We monitor all flight statuses. If your flight is delayed, your driver will automatically adjust the pickup time without any additional waiting charges.
